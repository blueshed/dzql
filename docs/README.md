# Docs

Two kinds. **Guidance**, current:

- `stories.md` -- what eta is for, and how stories drive it.
- `bento/` -- where am I and what can I do: the directions (`README.md`), built in `src/bento.ts`, and two sets of sample designs (`design/`, `paintbrush/`).
- `first-app.md` -- a first app, step by step: chat, then leave, in the order you meet the words.
- `least-resistance.md` -- why eta suits an AI that builds apps, what still slows it, and the
  five things that would make eta the easy path, model first among them.
- `plan-2026-10-14.md` -- the ten medium items, a story each, done by a team in parallel.
- `feedback-cafe-2026-10-14.md` -- what building the cafe on eta 0.53.0 found: nine frictions, each with a suggestion.
- `concise-skills.md` -- the standard the skills are cut to: a compass, not an encyclopedia.
- `eta-app-notes.md` -- the why behind the eta-app skill: the numbers, the reasons and the
  sketches (OAuth2, LDAP) that left it when it was cut.
- `review-2026-10-13.md` -- the review in progress: is the repo as small and simple as it can be,
  with every example and test justified. Its decisions are being carried out.

**The record**: finished work, kept for the record. Nothing in it is guidance: the current rules
are in `CLAUDE.md`, the eta-app skill, `stories.md` and `docs/bento/README.md`, and where these
disagree with them, they are right.

- `change-request.md` -- what the wedding asked of the kernel, eleven items, all done. Code comments
  that say "item N" mean these.
- `bento-request.md` -- the bento's rules built into the examples: kernel items K1-K3, then the
  wedding (W), chat (C) and the ticker (T), all done by 0.42.0. Its chat (C1: three compartments on
  a box) was replaced on 17 September 2026 by chat as three steps. Comments that say "K1", "W3" mean
  these.
- `review-2026-09-17.md` -- a first-principles review: is eta DRY, KISS, YAGNI; does it deliver its
  README; does it use Bun well.
- `chat-on-delta.md` -- the experiment that ran chat on @blueshed/delta beside the kernel, and what
  it found. Chat itself now keeps its documents in delta, and the experiment's example was retired
  on 24 September 2026.
