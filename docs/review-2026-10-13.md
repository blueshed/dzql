- **Use delta's code:** delta's `splitPath` and `applyOps` replace `json-pointer.ts`, `applyPatch` and parts' `without`.

- **The server:** the two write paths share their "it landed" tail and their permit check.
- **The CLI:** `extractFlag` replaces two copies of the same loop.
- **Exports only tests use:** `Assembled.draw` and `documents.names()`.
- **Long comments:** the benchmark notes on `send`, `handle`, and `db.ts`.

**Proof.**
- **One step shape:** a single `step()` for every phase.
- **The harness header:** 47 lines retell docs/stories.md; it can be 6.
- **Test helpers:** one `load(name)` for examples loaded by hand 20+ times, and helpers for the builders written 4 to 6 times.
- **Repeated story runs:** chat's in `harness.test`, leave's first story, and the `REVEAL=1` costs run.
- **Unused exports:** six exports no other file uses.

**Browser client.**
- **Tests:** 36 needless waits after `pushRender`, and a `sentAt()` helper for about 25 wait-and-expect pairs.
- **One answer per write:** one `answered(id, answer)` in place of `lastSent`, `sending` and `hearing`.
- **Repeated checks:** `isField` is written three times and the radio test four times.
- **Duplicate tests:** one shared test module for the app and tools tests.
- **Dead links:** comments still cite `bento-request.md` and `change-request.md`.

**Dead weight.**
- modeling's `icons.ts`, which nothing imports.
- Five page surfaces the wedding destructures and never uses.
- Two CSS rules that do nothing.

**Each example, justified.**
- **chat:** the smallest proof, the first walk in docs/first-app.md, and the CLI. Clean. Keep.
- **leave:** sign-in, drags done by hand, a manager's rules, and docs/first-app.md's second walk. Clean. Keep.
- **ticker:** the only box with a tray, a live feed, facts, an applet that takes patches, an island. 4 warnings (applets sent again whole, n115). Keep, and fix them.
- **wedding:** its own share is small: editing on blur, undo locked at a deadline, pictures, sign-in by code. But it is 3,500 lines of code and 2,300 of tests, with 1 warning. Keep what proves those; the rest is the showcase. **Your call.**
- **modeling, starter:** what `bun create` ships. Keep.
- **londons:** 149 stories and no app; only the modeller's tests read them. With the modeling app, they belong in a model, not `examples/`.
- **bento:** the README's page rules are the spec `CLAUDE.md` points to. The mock-up page beside them predates the kernel's `bento.ts`.
