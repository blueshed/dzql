# The Shed: stories

The allotment society keeps its tools in a shed and lends them to members. These are the stories
of the app that runs it, in five stages. Each is a sentence a member or the committee could call
true or false.

**How it is used.** Make a new app from this template. For each stage, in order, open a fresh
Claude Code session in the app and give it one line, *"Make these stories true:"*, followed by that
stage's stories. Earlier stages' stories must stay true. Give it nothing else: not this paragraph,
not `expected.md` (what each stage is checked against). The example is held out: it appears in no
skill or reference, so what the session makes is what the skills and libraries lead it to.

The people:

- **Sam** keeps the shed.
- **Ann** and **Ben** are members with plots in the north field.
- **Dot** is a member with a plot in the south field.
- **Cara** is on the committee.

## Stage 1: the shed's list

1. Sam adds a border spade to the shed's list, and Ann, who has the list open, sees it there
   without doing anything.
2. Sam renames the border spade to "long-handled spade", and every open list shows the new name.
3. Sam takes the broken rake off the list, and it goes from every open list.
4. A tool with no name is refused, with a message saying so, and nobody's list changes.

## Stage 2: borrowing

1. Ann picks the long-handled spade and the shears into her basket, and her basket shows both.
2. Ben's basket is his own: Ann's picks are not in it, and his are not in hers.
3. With Ann's basket open, Sam renames the shears "hedge shears"; Ann sees the new name in the
   list and in her basket.
4. Ann borrows what is in her basket: it becomes her loan, dated today, and her basket is empty.
5. Ann's loan is there when she comes back tomorrow, on another phone.

## Stage 3: the shed knows what is out

1. Sam's board shows every loan as it is made: Ann's, Ben's and Dot's.
2. A tool on loan shows as out on every member's list, and as in again once it is returned.
3. Ben cannot borrow the hedge shears while Ann has them; he is told they are out.
4. Ann returns the hedge shears, then undoes the return: the shears are out again, on her loan, on
   Sam's board and on every list.
5. Ann's undo takes back her own last change, never Ben's, even when Ben has changed something
   since.
6. Sam's board shows who has each tool, but not their phone number. Members' phone numbers are
   kept for the committee.

## Stage 4: kits and due dates

The app has been in use for a month, and members have tools out.

1. The strimmer becomes a kit of the strimmer, its battery and its charger, which are lent and
   returned together. A member borrowing the strimmer has all three on their loan.
2. A loan is due back two weeks from the day it was made, and every list says when.
3. The loans made before this change are due two weeks from their own day.
4. Nobody's loan is lost in the change, and nothing that is out shows as in.

## Stage 5: two sheds, and each member's own

The society opens a second shed at the south gate. It shares the same tools.

1. A tool lent at the south gate shows as out at the north shed, and the other way round. Each shed
   runs on its own server.
2. Ann sees her own loans and no one else's. Ben sees his own.
3. Ann and Ben, both in the north field, each see the north field's loans, and not the south
   field's. Dot sees the south field's.
4. Cara, on the committee, sees every loan, and members' phone numbers.
5. Someone who is not a member sees the shed's list, and no loans.
6. Everything that was true at the north shed is still true, at both sheds.
